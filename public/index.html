Football

Goal: two rows of five equal square openings, nine black bars.

Simulated goal probability is loading. Four LED positions: X G and probability. Hold the football to aim when ready; release to freeze the shot estimate. Monte Carlo physics estimate without a goalkeeper, not match-statistics xG.

Press and hold the football to charge. While holding, move toward one of the ten goal openings to set the shot direction, then release to shoot. The ball is judged only by its real path. On a keyboard: focus the football, hold Space or Enter to charge, use the arrow keys to aim (Shift for fine aim), release to shoot, and press R to reset.
